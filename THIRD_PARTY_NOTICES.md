## Hermes Agent — MIT License

Adapted in:

- `app/faltbitte.py` — the ceilings on what a compaction request carries: a
  tool call's arguments and its result (`_serialize_for_summary`), and the
  ceiling over the whole request, cut from the middle with a visible note
  (`_bound_summary_input`), and the room the summary keeps in the window
  (`_MIN_SUMMARY_TOKENS`, `_SUMMARY_TOKENS_CEILING`, 5 % of the window).

  `agent/prompt_builder.py`: `TASK_COMPLETION_GUIDANCE` and
  `TOOL_USE_ENFORCEMENT_GUIDANCE` word for word, and
  `OPENAI_MODEL_EXECUTION_GUIDANCE` cut into its lines with this program's
  tool names, each line dropped where its tool is not in the run (as
  `execution_guidance_text` does for `web_search`).
